| Story and structured editing | Existing authoring context and operations | Describe alignment, precise targeting, revisions and inspectable changes | Automatic narrative generation, guaranteed obedience, automatic aesthetic approval or freedom from visual review |
| Possible video/image-editing expansion | Unformed future ideas explicitly deferred by the author | Keep out of this introduction unless later requested | Current features or a committed roadmap |

Do not make the author read a long disclaimer on every page. Preserve scope through accurate wording, first-person voice, suitable source attribution and notes. Put test limitations and delivery accounting in supporting records unless they are necessary to interpret a claim in the talk.

## Shared hard rules for authored objects

1. Use a native table object when the meaning is genuinely two-dimensional: comparable attributes across row entities. Do not emulate cells using many text boxes, rectangular shapes, hand-drawn rules, cards or components. Renaming a fake table does not change the rule.
2. Use one rich-text object for an ordinary paragraph or list where practical. Use internal paragraphs, bullets and selective emphasis. Separate objects need a real reading, evidence or interaction purpose; do not split every phrase merely to align a layout.
3. Preserve purposeful scientific multi-panel figures, aligned plots, labels attached to evidence and meaningful diagram nodes. Their geometry carries a comparison or relationship. This rule does not flatten evidence or prohibit boxes and arrows as a category.
4. Preserve original cited figure/table pixels where the source itself is evidence. Do not use a generated image of a fake table to evade editable-object requirements.
5. Use real UI captures and actual page previews for product state. A conceptual diagram is permitted when its relationships are accurate and it is not presented as a screenshot.
6. Keep original logos, imagery, source values and citations truthful. Use a coherent visual system without making blankness, whiteness, low object count or diagnostic count the definition of human authorship.
7. Keep authoring independence. ChatGPT and DeepSeek must not use Claude's previous deck as an aesthetic reference. Client-specific feedback is delivered only to that client; shared source facts and hard rules are the common input.

## Story and script acceptance

Use the runtime's existing Story schema. Main Story carries the overall purpose, audience and takeaway; Presentation Story adjusts these to the talk. Page/local Story is the page's designIntent, with its contribution, requested change and preservation constraints. Section Story supports a useful grouping. Do not add a parallel outline database or invent new required fields.

Develop the speaking explanation alongside the pages. Notes should be usable sentences in the author's voice, with enough context to explain the example and a transition to what follows. They should not be a copy of on-screen labels or an after-the-fact marketing script. These are authoring requirements for this task, not a mandatory form-filling gate built into the product.

A complete speaker script may be derived from the original account and refined for the final presentation. Do not claim that HUANDENG automatically converts a recording into slides or generates that script without an actual supported workflow.
